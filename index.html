HOSPITALITY & CAMPUS SERVICES

Welcome to the dy Patil campus. Use this digital survival toolkit to navigate our hostels, mess dining, safety, and local services during the event.

For comfortable residential living, keep these rules in mind:

  • Hostel Registration: Bring copy of payment slip and verification slips to Block A desk.
  • Curfew Hours: Entry and exit must be updated by 09:30 PM daily.
  • Quiet Hours: Post 10:00 PM, quiet hours are enforced in the hallways to assist students who are studying or sleeping.

Rotating hot meals are served in the Mess Complex daily:

  • Breakfast: 07:30 AM - 09:15 AM
  • Lunch: 12:30 PM - 02:15 PM
  • High Tea: 05:00 PM - 06:15 PM
  • Dinner: 07:45 PM - 09:30 PM

Your safety is our top priority. The campus is equipped with 24/7 security and a medical emergency center:

  • Medical Room: Located in the Student Wellness Hub (near Admin Block). Open 24/7 with a doctor on call.
  • Security Hotlines: Security guards are posted at every block gate. If you spot suspicious activity, dial the security hotline (found in the POC block).
  • ID Cards: Always carry your physical student ID card. Security checks are standard at the main university entry gate.

Welcome to Pune! Here are some local tips to help you settle in:

  • Transport: Auto-rickshaws (Ola, Uber, or local meter) are easily accessible outside the main gate. The nearest major bus depot is Lohegaon.
  • Food Outlets: While the mess serves three hot meals a day, check out DY Patil College Road for local cafes, street food, and essential stationery shops.
  • Weather: Pune weather is pleasant, but carrying an umbrella during August orientation is highly recommended as sudden monsoons are frequent.
